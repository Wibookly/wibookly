# Architecture Rules

- Preserve legacy EnergyForward domains, sender addresses, storage keys, package identifiers, and super-admin identity until replacement Nikkore infrastructure is verified; these are operational identifiers, not product branding.
- Nikkore Inbox is the platform brand; organization names and logos remain tenant-owned and must be scoped by `organization_id`.
- Personal signups receive an isolated personal organization; organization signups may join only explicitly allowlisted company domains.
